Questions

What is hustless?

hustless is an AI spending coach for iPhone and iPad that helps you stop impulse buying. When you are about to buy something, you tell it what it is, and it asks one short question at a time so you can decide whether you really want it. It also logs your spending by voice or text, reminds you before subscriptions and bills, and shows where your money goes.

Is hustless a budgeting app?

Not in the strict sense. There is no budget to stick to and no guilt. hustless tracks your spending and fixed costs, but its focus is the moment before a purchase: helping you buy what is worth it to you.

Does the AI tell me what to buy?

No. It never recommends products, shops or brands, and it never tells you what to do. It asks questions, and when you decide, it respects your choice.

How does hustless help with impulse buying?

Impulse purchases happen fast. hustless adds a short pause: a few calm questions about why you want the item, what you already have and whether it fits. If you choose to wait, it suggests giving it 48 hours — if you still remember it, you probably want it.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your expenses stay on your devices and, if you turn it on, sync through your own private iCloud. Only the AI features send what they need to answer, and conversations are not stored on our server.

How much does hustless cost?

It starts with a 7-day free trial through the App Store. After that it is $3.49 a month or $34.99 a year in the United States; prices in other countries are shown in the App Store. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Do I pay separately for iPhone and iPad?

No. hustless is one app for iPhone and iPad, and the subscription belongs to your Apple ID. Subscribe once and it works on both, with your data in sync through your own iCloud.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, in sync through iCloud. The app is available in English, Greek and Japanese, with light and dark mode and adjustable text size.

Where is hustless available?

hustless is coming to the App Store in 145 countries and regions. It is not yet available in the European Union.

How do I cancel?

In the app, go to You → Account → Manage subscription, or on your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. More help is on the support page.
